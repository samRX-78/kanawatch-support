# KanaWatch Support

KanaWatch is a standalone Apple Watch app for practicing Japanese hiragana and katakana.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Does KanaWatch require an iPhone?**
No. KanaWatch is fully standalone and works independently on your Apple Watch.

**How does the mastery tracking work?**
A character is marked as mastered once you've practiced it at least 3 times with 70% or more accuracy. Progress is saved automatically and persists between sessions.

**How do I reset my progress?**
Go to Settings within the app and select the option to clear your progress.
